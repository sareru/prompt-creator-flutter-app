# prompt_creator_flutter_app

Turning my [BL Prompt Creator](https://sareru.net/promptcreator/) for creative writing and art from a simple website into a free mobile app for everyone.\
This app will also be available on the usual mobile app stores in the future.\
*This app in still in beta and has only been tested on iOS.*

![Summary graphic](https://sareru.net/promptcreator/app-sc.png)

## Current features

- Create story prompts
- Share your favorite prompts on social media
- View and manage your list of generated story prompts
- Search in your list of story prompts
- Add notes to your story prompts
- Light/dark mode

## Never coming feature

- genAI

Writing prompts should tickle your brain and make you use your own creativity and imagination. genAI takes this away from you and ruins all the fun and then some.

## Contributions

If you would like to contribute to this project, feel free to add to it!\
Especially more additions to the prompts are always welcome.\
You can find all the available possibilities (traits, jobs, actions and places) in `lib/helpers/vars.dart`
